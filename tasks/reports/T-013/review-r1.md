## Declared deviations

I accept all of them. Per-term stretch matching is stricter than the literal spec: squeezing runs to 2 would miss `fuuuck` → `fuuck`. `ass` ≠ `as` is tested. The repetition rule skipping one-character words is fine, because the LLM layer still sees `a a a a`. Treating `approve` with a non-`ok` reason as a reject, and letting refusal always win, both fail closed. The `setTimeout` + `AbortController` timeout covers `response.text()`: an abort during the body read lands in the catch with `signal.aborted` → `timeout`. For the shared `test-kit.ts`, see R1-05.

**Fail-closed audit:** timeout, network, every non-2xx, a non-JSON 200, a schema-invalid body, `incomplete`/`failed`/unknown status, a missing message, non-JSON or schema-invalid model text, and unknown enums all end in `transient` or a reject. A 400 from an unsupported `reasoning.effort` maps to `bad_request` (error log, transient), so it can't publish. **Injection containment:** `<`/`>` are escaped, so the visitor can't produce a real `</visitor_note>`. The developer message is constant (tested over all 10 injection fixtures). `locale` comes from a validated enum. Port names and unions match the task, ADR-0005, and §6.
