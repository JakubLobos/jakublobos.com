## Notes for orchestrator

- **Doc drift:** `docs/design-system.md` §2 doesn't list the CR-1 "controls" tokens, yet `tokens.css` cites §2 as the reference. Add a short §2.9 "Controls" table (shared surface, orchestrator-owned).
- **Please confirm the `disabled` reading.** The task note says "Disabled and busy states keep the design's _sending_ appearance". The implementer instead maps `disabled` to the design's "sent" look (yellow, `aria-disabled`, still focusable), which is the design's only `aria-disabled` button. I think this is the right call for fidelity, but `disabled` doesn't block activation. **T-030** must keep the sent button `type="button"` (as in the design) and guard its own submit handler. **T-029/T-030** also have to toggle `data-busy`, `data-done`, `disabled`, `aria-busy` and `aria-disabled` at runtime, because the component is static markup. Worth one line in those task files.
